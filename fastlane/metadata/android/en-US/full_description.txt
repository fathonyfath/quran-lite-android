<p>With <i>Qur’an Lite</i> you can read the Qur’an on your Android smartphone or tablet. App size is kept as small as possible without sacrificing functionality, content is fetched by the app after install.</p><p>The app offers a Light and Dark theme and prefetches all the contents. More features are planned and will become available with new versions, like searching for a Surah or bookmark your last reading page.</p>
